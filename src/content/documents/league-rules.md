These points are taken from the 2024, 2025 and 2026 AGMs and the August 2026 committee meeting. The full match rules captains use on the night are also on [League Master](https://bedfordshiresquash.leaguemaster.co.uk/).

## Scoring

- **Division 1** is played **point-a-rally (PAR) to 11**, best of five games. The 2024 AGM voted for this (11 in favour, 1 against) and later AGMs have kept it.
- **Division 2** is played **point-a-rally (PAR) to 15**, best of five games.
- On cold courts, players may use a single-dot ball if they prefer.

## Order of play

- Order follows **SquashLevels**, with a tolerance of **250 points**.
- A player may play up to **three matches** above their place in either half of the season.
- **Division 2** has a SquashLevels cap of **2500**, set at the start of the season. The league organiser flags exceptions.

## Fees

Unchanged at the 2026 AGM:

- **Winter:** £80 and £50
- **Summer:** £50 per team

## Rearrangements

If a team asks to rearrange a fixture and then wins that match, the five bonus points for the win are removed.

## Teams

The 2026 AGM recorded eight teams in each division, with a similar number expected this season. Fixtures, results and tables are on [League Master](https://bedfordshiresquash.leaguemaster.co.uk/).
