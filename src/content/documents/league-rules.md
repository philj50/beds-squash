## Scoring

- **Division 1** is played **point-a-rally (PAR) to 11**, best of five games.
- **Division 2** is played **point-a-rally (PAR) to 15**, best of five games.
- Fixtures, results and league tables are managed on [League Master](https://bedfordshiresquash.leaguemaster.co.uk/).
