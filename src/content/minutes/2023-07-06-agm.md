## Report from the Chairman

A lot of positive change, especially juniors, yearly event was great, committee is good, but is always open for improvement.

Would like some ideas going forward on how to improve squash for the county, open to suggestions.


## Report from the County Captain

O55 - nearly won national competition, lost to Herts in the final narrowly.

Senior side - good run, nearly got to the final weekend.
