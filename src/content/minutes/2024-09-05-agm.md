Failure to play fixture penalties – Chris Moss

For any team that wants a rearrangement of a fixture there would be the removal of the 5 points in the event of a subsequent team win by the team that wanted the re-arrangement.

For Example: Team A wants to re-arrange the fixture and agrees with Team B to do so. They then play the fixture and Team A win 4:1 (All 3:1s), they would receive 12 points for all of the individual wins and the extra point for the 3:1 loss, but not the 5 bonus points, so the overall score would be 13:7, NOT 18:7. In the system this would read as 18:7 with 5 penalty points applied, manually by the league organiser.

Seconded by Marcus
