## Minutes of the 2024 AGM

## Report from the Chairman

Great year overall, everyone very enthusiastic and Bedfordshire Squash is definitely on the rise in Squash, lovely to see, be a part of and look forward to the coming year.

Thanks to the committee for all of their effort this year.

Special Thanks given to Gary (Junior Coach) for his work and contribution.

Very positive to see strong junior growth.


## Report from the County Captain

O55 team did not run this year, but Ian Thomas will run the O55 team in the coming year

Open age group results from last year

Beat Kent 3:2

Lost to Herts 5:0

Narrowly lost to Northants 2:3


## Report from the Treasurer

Accounts increased from £3,000 to £4,500 as of today

> Outstanding action: Treasurer to investigate why Beds Closed revenue was down compared to previous year


## Report from Juniors Team Manager

County tournament entries increased from 30 to 42 across categories

Junior Beds Closed set for 9th November 2025

Academy at Mosebury performing well

Beds Schools event grew from 40 to 50 entries

Development squash progressing positively

Tri-County Cup with Cambs and Northants continues to be a success


## Report from Beds Closed – Simon Marshall / Steve Davies

80 entrants across A, B, C categories

No ladies’ entrants this year, which was a shame

Proposal: offer Ladies A, B, C categories to encourage entries

Thursday evening matches required last year, but worked well

TV coverage was excellent – will continue

Automatic entry system worked well and will be used again

2026 event scheduled for 8th–11th January


## Report from League Organiser

2025 Winners: OU1 (Div 1) and Bulls (Div 2)

Div 1 PAR 11 scoring worked well and will continue

2025/26 season to start 2nd week of October 2025

Suggestion accepted: for cold courts, players may use single dot ball if preferred

No complaints received this year (a positive outcome)

Cap on Div 2 set at 2500 on Squash Levels
