Based at the Lewsey Squash Centre, L&D has access to the most courts in Bedfordshire. The club runs active internal ladders, a league team and a development team of mixed abilities that plays friendlies against clubs across the region.

L&D aims to raise the profile of squash in Luton and Dunstable by welcoming new players, encouraging existing players to play more and bringing lapsed players back to the game.
