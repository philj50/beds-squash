Luton & Dunstable now play at Houghton Regis Leisure & Community Centre. The club runs active internal ladders, a league team and a development team of mixed abilities that plays friendlies against clubs across the region. Pay-and-play courts remain at [Lewsey Sports Park](/clubs/lewsey-sports-park/).

L&D aims to raise the profile of squash in Luton and Dunstable by welcoming new players, encouraging existing players to play more and bringing lapsed players back to the game.
