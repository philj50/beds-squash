Four heated block-and-plaster courts with sprung floors, glass back walls and LED lighting, plus a viewing gallery. Home of the **Bedfordshire County Closed Championships** and the largest club programme in the county, with adult and junior coaching, box leagues and social squash.

Club Towers enters four teams into the Bedfordshire leagues (Monday and Tuesday evenings), a team in the Cambridgeshire league, and racketball teams in the Northants spring league.
