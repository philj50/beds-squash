Bedford Bulls play out of the Mowsbury Golf & Squash Centre on the northern edge of Bedford. Three glass-backed courts with a spectator area. Courts are pay-and-play through Better (GLL), so it is an easy place to try squash for the first time.

The Bulls run three teams in the Bedfordshire leagues and junior coaching also takes place at Mowsbury.
