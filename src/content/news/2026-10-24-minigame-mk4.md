[Minigame mk4](/minigame/) is the game on the site now. You look down on the court.

A word on the floor tells you the shot. **Straight** stays on the side the ball came from. **Cross court** has to go to the other half. **Boast** has to touch the side wall on the way to the front. Take the ball on the edge of the racket for the boast, and nearer the middle for the other two.

The ball then has to pitch in that back corner. The box lights up when it does. Before the ball crosses the short line, the racket has to be back inside the circle on the T. Stuck in the corner, and the ball runs past.

The score is a match. You against the court, first to 11. At 10-all you need two clear. A good length is your point. A miss, the wrong shot, or a late return to the T is a point to the court.

The board beside the court has started again. Mk3 scores are still stored, and they are not listed. A game to 11 cannot sit next to a high score of 1167. A win comes first. 11–0 beats 11–9. The ten best results are the ones that show.
