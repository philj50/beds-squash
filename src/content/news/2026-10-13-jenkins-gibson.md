Charlie Jenkins beat David Gibson in Division 2 of the Winter 2026/27 on 13 October 2026.

Charlie Jenkins plays for Club Towers 4 and David Gibson for Open University 2. Open University 2 were at home. The rubber went to a fifth game, and Jenkins won it 17-15.

That rubber won the fixture for Club Towers 4. The side that wins more rubbers takes five points, and those five points go to Club Towers 4.
