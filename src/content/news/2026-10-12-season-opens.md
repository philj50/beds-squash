The Winter 2026/27 league has its first result on the books.

Shenley Leisure 1 beat Bedford Bulls 1 16–7 in Division 1 on 12 October 2026. Shenley won three rubbers to two, and the games finished 11–7. League points are one for each game, plus five for the match.

The rubbers: Declan Osborn beat Matthew Nankivell 3–0. Dan Evans beat Samuel Morris 3–0. Thomas Parker beat Chris Plummer 3–2. Nick Cartwright beat Cameron Mackay 3–1. Lewis Noble beat Miles Evans 3–0.

The scorecard is on the [league tables](/leagues/results/#2/2/t12/m4).
