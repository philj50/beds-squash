[CM Stringers](https://www.cmstringers.com/entry/) last updated its tournament diary on Sunday 18 October 2026, at 6:13 pm. The new listing is the Harrogate Squash Open on 12 December. Every event is marked entry not open, so the closing dates below are the ones published on the page. Check that page before trying to enter.

The nearest events for Bedfordshire players are Harrow and Radlett.

- **Sunday 8 November** — The Harrow Open, Harrow Leisure Centre, Christchurch Avenue, Harrow, HA3 5BD. Entries close on Sunday 1 November. The fee is £22.50.
- **Saturday 21 November** — The Radlett Classic, Radlett Lawn Tennis & Squash Club, 425 Watling Street, Radlett, WD7 7JG. Entries close on Sunday 15 November.

## October

The 17 October Uxbridge Oktober Classic is no longer on the diary.

- **Saturday 24 October** — The Purley Open, Purley Sports Club, The Ridge, Purley, CR8 3PF. Entries close on Sunday 18 October.
- **Sunday 25 October** — The CM Coolhurst Junior Graded, Coolhurst Tennis & Squash Club, Coolhurst Road, Crouch End, N8 8EY. SquashLevels 0–2,000. Entries close on Sunday 18 October. The fee is £20, and free for MJS members.
- **Saturday 31 October to Sunday 1 November** — The Cumberland Classic, Cumberland Lawn Tennis Club, 25 Alvanley Gardens, London, NW6 1JD. Entries close on Sunday 25 October. SquashLevels 0–20,000.

## November and December

- **Saturday 7 November** — Ali Quealy Squash Open, Rodmersham Squash & Fitness Club, Kent. This one adds an F grade. Entries close on Sunday 1 November.
- **Saturday 7 November** — Titan Squash Series, The Weald Racquets Club, Hassocks. Entries close on Sunday 1 November.
- **Saturday 14 to Sunday 15 November** — The Wroclaw Classic, a graded squash and racketball weekend in Wrocław. Entries close on Sunday 1 November.
- **Saturday 21 November** — The Chichester Graded, Chichester Racquets & Fitness Club. Entries close on Sunday 15 November.
- **Saturday 28 November** — The Dulwich Classic, Dulwich Sports Club. Entries close on Sunday 22 November. The fee is £32.50.
- **Sunday 29 November** — The Bromley LTC Open, Bromley Lawn Tennis & Squash Club. Entries close on Sunday 22 November. The fee is £32.50.
- **Saturday 5 December** — The Connaught Open, The Connaught Club, Chingford. Entries close on Sunday 29 November.
- **Friday 4 to Saturday 5 December** — The Kent County Closed Championships, Bexley Lawn Tennis, Squash & Rball Club. Squash and racketball. Entries close on Sunday 29 November.
- **Saturday 12 December** — The Canterbury Xmas Doubles, Canterbury Squash Rackets Club. Entries close on Sunday 5 December.
- **Saturday 12 December** — The Harrogate Squash Open, a Pro-Am graded event at Harrogate Sports & Fitness Club, Hookstone Wood Rd, Harrogate, HG2 8PN. Categories are Pro Grade and grades A to E, for SquashLevels 0–45,000. The diary lists £2000 prize money for the Pro Grade. Entries close on Sunday 5 December. The fee is £32.50 and includes an event shirt. The club website is printed as hwww.harrogatesfc.co.uk, with an extra h at the start.
- **Saturday 19 December** — The Lindgate Classic, Lincoln, at Lindum Sports Association and Eastgate Tennis, Bowls and Squash Club. This one adds an F grade. Entries close on Sunday 13 December. The fee is £40.

## May 2027

**Saturday 8 to Sunday 9 May** — The Amsterdam Classic IV, a graded squash and racketball weekend. The diary gives Sunday 25 April 2026 as the closing date, which has already passed, and entry is marked not open.

Unless a fee is given above, the one-day events are £30 and include an event shirt. Most are grades A to E, for SquashLevels 0–15,000. Organisers and entry forms are on the [CM Stringers entry page](https://www.cmstringers.com/entry/).
