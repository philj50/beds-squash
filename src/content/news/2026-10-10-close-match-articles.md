League news no longer waits for someone to sit down and write it.

Each morning the site looks at the rubbers played the day before. It keeps the ones where both players have a SquashLevels rating. If nobody produced a clear upset, it chooses the closest match: the two players whose ratings were nearest to each other.

That rubber becomes one news story. It names the winner and the loser, the teams, the division, and the games. It records the SquashLevels marks before the match, and the marks afterwards when those are known. It adds the fixture points, and a link to the scorecard on the [league tables](/leagues/results/).

There is one story for the day, and only on a day when somebody played. The wording stays with the facts. An admin can let that story publish on its own, or hold it back, from Match articles in Administration.

A big upset takes the place of the close match. That choice is described in [Outsider wins get their own story](/news/2026-10-10-outsider-win-articles/).
