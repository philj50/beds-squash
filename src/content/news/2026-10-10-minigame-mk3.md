[Minigame mk3](/minigame/) is the new one-player game, looking down the court.

The ball no longer comes straight down the middle. It leaves the front wall at an angle, and it often runs to a side wall on the way back. Hit it before it bounces twice. The racket still stays behind the service line.

A shot off the edge of the racket is a boast: it goes to the side wall first, then on to the front. A shot from the middle of the racket goes more directly at the front wall.

The ball changes colour through the rally. It can be orange, purple, green, blue, pink or yellow.

Bonuses pop up across the court, not only in the middle:

- A pumpkin is worth 10 and stays a little longer.
- A skull is worth 20.
- A bat is worth 30. Bats sit nearer the side walls, or close to the front, and they do not stay long.

The page is dressed for Halloween, with cobwebs, skeletons and lanterns. A short tune starts when you press Play. At the end of the game a ghost appears over the court.

Type your initials, press Play, then Serve. Scores still go on the board beside the court.
