After 140 matches involving over 100 players across four days, Bedfordshire's latest squash champions have been crowned at Club Towers.

For the first time ever there were two Ladies grades, alongside Open A, B and C grades, an Over 55s tournament and plate competitions for first-round losers.

## Open A Grade

Top seed **Gbenga Adeyi** won his fourth straight A Grade title, beating Club Towers' **Steve Davies** 11-6, 11-5, 11-9 in the final. Davies had earlier upset club-mate and last year's finalist Ben Mann in the semi-final.

## Ladies A Grade

Two thrilling five-game semi-finals set up the final. **Rachael Hamp** edged Jolanta Hawryluk 12-10 in the fifth, while **Ashleigh Kemp** upset second seed Sophie Cable 11-9 in the decider. An injury to Hamp in the final handed Kemp her first county title.

## Other grades

- **Open B Grade:** Phil Jenkins (Club Towers) beat Karan Dhokia (Open University) 3-1
- **Ladies B Grade:** Anne Mildon beat Karen Ford 15-4, 15-4, 15-4 without dropping a game all weekend
- **Open C Grade:** Barry Smith (Bedford Bulls) beat Richard Fryer 16-14, 15-10, 15-11
- **Over 55s:** Ian Thomas retained his title against Matthew Towers
- **Plates:** Sam Gelder (A), Charlie Jessop (B), Ben Robinson (C)
