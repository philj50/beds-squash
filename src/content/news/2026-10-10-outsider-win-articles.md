A close match is a good read. An outsider win is a better one.

Before the site writes the daily league story, it looks for a rubber where the winner went in with the lower SquashLevels rating, and the gap was large: at least 200 points, or at least 12 percent of the higher rating. If several rubbers qualify, the widest gap is the one that is published.

The story is the same shape as any other match report. It names both players and their teams, the games, and the ratings before and after. It says, in one line, that the winner came from the lower rating. The scorecard is linked from the [league tables](/leagues/results/).

If no rubber clears that bar, the day falls back to the closest match instead. That is covered in [Close matches are written up on their own](/news/2026-10-10-close-match-articles/).

The ratings behind the choice are the same figures on the [SquashLevels](/leagues/ratings/) page.
