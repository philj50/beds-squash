England Squash Masters has asked counties to pass on the remaining events for the 2026-27 season to over-35 players.

New female members still get free entry to their first regional or bronze event. Register for the event, then email [events@englandsquashmasters.co.uk](mailto:events@englandsquashmasters.co.uk?subject=Regional%20Event%20-%20Free%20Entry%20Fee%20Claim) with the name used on the entry.

## Next event

**Karakal East of England Regional** — Friday 30 October to Sunday 1 November 2026, at Grantham Squash and Fitness Centre, Harlaxton Road, Grantham, NG31 7JX.

Entries close at 6pm on Friday 16 October 2026. [Event details and entry](https://www.englandsquashmasters.co.uk/Events/EventDetails.aspx?@EventID=161).

## Later this season

- **South of England** — Friday 27 to Sunday 29 November 2026, Berkhamsted Lawn Tennis & Squash Rackets Club, Lower Kings Road, Berkhamsted, HP4 2AL. [Details](https://www.englandsquashmasters.co.uk/Events/EventDetails.aspx?@EventID=162).
- **Midlands** — Friday 15 to Sunday 17 January 2027, Wolverhampton Lawn Tennis & Squash Club, Newbridge Crescent, Wolverhampton, WV6 0LH. [Details](https://www.englandsquashmasters.co.uk/Events/EventDetails.aspx?@EventID=163).
- **3 Counties Squash Masters Open** (bronze) — Friday 29 to Sunday 31 January 2027, Tring Squash Club, Pendley Sports Centre, Cow Lane, Tring, HP23 5NS. [Details](https://www.englandsquashmasters.co.uk/Events/EventDetails.aspx?@EventID=166).
- **British National Squash Championships 2027** — Wednesday 17 to Sunday 21 February 2027, Colets Health & Fitness Club, St Nicholas Road, Thames Ditton, KT7 0PW. [Details](https://www.englandsquashmasters.co.uk/Events/EventDetails.aspx?@EventID=164).

Questions go to Brian Brock, the event manager, on 07757 739156 or [events@englandsquashmasters.co.uk](mailto:events@englandsquashmasters.co.uk). The full list is on the [England Squash Masters](https://www.englandsquashmasters.co.uk/) site.
