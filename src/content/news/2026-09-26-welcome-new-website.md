Welcome to the new home of squash and racketball in Bedfordshire.

The site replaces the old Beds SRA website and brings everything together:

- **[Clubs & courts](/clubs/)** — an interactive map of every club and pay-and-play venue in the county, with teams, court counts and contact details.
- **[Events](/events/)** — county tournaments, league dates, junior sessions and key England Squash dates, with a calendar feed you can subscribe to.
- **[Leagues](/leagues/)** — rules and a direct link to fixtures and tables on League Master.
- **[Tournaments & Honours](/tournaments/)** — the County Closed, League Cup and the honours board.
- **[Documents](/documents/)** — AGM and committee minutes, the constitution, safeguarding policies and codes of conduct.
- **[Gallery](/gallery/)** — photos from county events.

The site is only as good as the content on it. If you have club news, a tournament to promote, team reports or photos, please [get in touch](/contact/).
