The site can take what you saw at the club, without a separate email.

Sign in and open [Send something in](/share/). You can send a photo, a short video, a link, or an article. The name on it is yours: the League Master name when you have one, otherwise the name on your profile. You do not type a credit, and the site ignores a different name sent another way.

Nothing appears in public on its own. An admin publishes it from Administration. Photos and videos then show in the [gallery](/gallery/). Articles show under Sent in on the [news](/news/) page.

My Squash lists the articles sent from your login. If you are one of the three people with the most published articles, that list carries stars.
