My Squash is the page for the person who is signed in. It will sit in the menu after News.

The top of the page is your next match, and the club where you need to be. When the club is on the site, that includes the address.

If you are a player, you see the previous team score. If you were on that sheet, you also see who you played and the score.

If you captain a team or a club, you see the earlier scores for that team, who is likely to play, and the SquashLevels gap from last time. The page then says whether that likely side looks stronger, similar, or weaker than the side that played last time.

Under that:

- The last two results, with the scores.
- A graph of your SquashLevels, point by point.
- The articles you have sent in. The three people with the most published articles get stars: three for first, two for second, one for third.

[Open My Squash](/captains/you/) once you are signed in. Your name, email and League Master place stay at the bottom of the page, with a link to your profile if you need to change a password.
