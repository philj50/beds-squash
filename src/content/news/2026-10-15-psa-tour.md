The events page now lists the next PSA Squash Tour events in England, taken from the public PSA calendar.

- **London Open** — 4–8 November 2026, London. Prize money $18,000.
- **GMS Accountants Sports Academy Open** — 6–8 November 2026, Cambridgeshire. Prize money $1,426.
- **PSA World Championships** — 27 March to 4 April 2027, London. Prize money $710,000.
- **British Open** — 26 May to 6 June 2027, Birmingham. A Diamond event. Prize money $371,500.

Each date links to its page on the PSA site. The same part of the events page shows the latest highlight from the SquashTV YouTube channel. Live matches stay on SquashTV.

The list is checked once a day. It changes when the public calendar or the highlights change.

The dates are on the [events page](/events/#psa-tour). The [PSA calendar](https://www.psasquashtour.com/tournaments/), the [SquashTV schedule](https://www.squash.tv/schedule/) and the [free highlights](https://www.squash.tv/free-for-you/) are linked there too.
