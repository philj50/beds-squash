The Bedfordshire Winter League returns for the 2026/27 season. The September AGM kept the same shape as last year: Division 1 (PAR to 11) and Division 2 (PAR to 15, SquashLevels cap 2500), eight teams in each division, and the same entry fees (£80 and £50). Fixtures, results and tables are on [League Master](https://bedfordshiresquash.leaguemaster.co.uk/).

Want to play team squash? [Contact your nearest club](/clubs/) — most teams are looking for players.
