Sunday 8th November, 10am–5pm at Towers Health & Racquets Club. Entry is free. Entries close on Wednesday 28th October. A parent or guardian can [enter a player](/juniors/closed/). The [poster](/uploads/juniors/beds-junior-county-closed-2026.pdf) is the same sheet as the printed flyer.

Age categories are Mixed U9, Girls U11, U13, U15, U17 and U19, and Boys U11, U13, U15, U17 and U19. The age group is worked out from the date of birth, using age on tournament date. The categories we run, and the format, depend on the number of entries.
