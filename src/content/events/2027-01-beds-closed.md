Entries are open. The fee is £22, and they close on Monday 14 December 2026. Enter by Wednesday 25 November for a guaranteed T-shirt.

Play is at Club Towers from Thursday 7 to Sunday 10 January. Thursday matches start at 5.45pm or later. The grades are A, Ladies A, Ladies B, B, C and Over 55s. A player enters one grade. Plates run where there are enough entries. The tournament is for Bedfordshire and Bedfordshire League players.

Play is PAR to 11. Indicative SquashLevels are A 2500 and above, B 1001–2499, and C up to 1000. The ladies grades are set from the entries. Streaming will run again, and the shirt sponsors are still in place.
