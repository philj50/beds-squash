The County Closed returns to Club Towers from **Thursday 7 to Sunday 10 January**. Play is PAR to 11 across the grades. Streaming will run again, and the shirt sponsors are still in place.

Last year’s event had more than 100 entries. The entry form will be published here when it is ready.
