## County squad programme

Bedfordshire runs a county junior squad for players who want to develop beyond club coaching and represent the county. Squad sessions are led by qualified coaches and follow the England Squash junior pathway.

> **Committee:** add current squad dates, venue, cost and how to be selected. The old site's *Junior Handbook*, *Squad Programme* and *Phase 1 Skill Challenge* documents can be uploaded under [Documents](/documents/).

## Junior county ladder

The junior ladder gives players regular competitive matches against others of a similar standard across the county.

## Inter-County Championships

Bedfordshire enters junior teams in the England Squash Inter-County Championships. Selection is from the county squad and ladder.

## Where to start

Most clubs run junior coaching — see [Club Towers](/clubs/club-towers/), [Bedford Bulls](/clubs/bedford-bulls/) and [Luton & Dunstable](/clubs/luton-and-dunstable/) to begin with, or [find your nearest courts](/clubs/).

## Safeguarding

All county junior activity follows our [Child Protection Policy](/documents/) and the Beds SRA codes of conduct for coaches, juniors and parents.
