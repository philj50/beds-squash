## Winter League

The main league runs from October to March with two divisions:

| Division | Scoring | Match nights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Division 1 | PAR to 11, best of 5 | Monday / Tuesday |
| Division 2 | PAR to 15, best of 5 | Monday / Tuesday |

## Summer League

A shorter, more relaxed league between May and August — ideal for new teams and players stepping up from club box leagues.

## League Cup

A knockout cup competition open to all league teams, with the final usually played in the spring.

## Fixtures, results & tables

All fixtures, results and league tables live on **League Master**. Team captains enter results there after each match.
