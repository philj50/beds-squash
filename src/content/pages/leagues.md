## Winter League

The main league runs through the winter with two divisions. The September 2026 AGM confirmed eight teams in each division again this season, on the same entry fees: **£80 and £50**.

| Division | Scoring | Order of play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Division 1 | PAR to 11, best of 5 | SquashLevels order, within 250 points |
| Division 2 | PAR to 15, best of 5 | SquashLevels order, within 250 points. Cap of 2500 |

## Summer League

A shorter league between May and August. Entry is **£50 per team**.

## League Cup

A knockout cup competition open to all league teams, with the final usually played in the spring.

## Recent winners

The 2026 AGM recorded the 2025/26 winners as **Shenley** (Division 1) and **Open University 2** (Division 2). The 2025 AGM recorded that season’s winners as **OU1** (Division 1) and **Bulls** (Division 2).

## Fixtures, results & tables

Winter and summer tables and fixtures are published on this site. The pages are for reading: team captains enter results on **League Master**, and those results are copied across.
