## What we do

- Run the **Bedfordshire Winter and Summer Leagues** and the **League Cup**
- Organise the **County Closed Championships** and other county tournaments
- Field **county teams** in the England Squash Inter-County Championships (Seniors, Masters and Juniors)
- Support **junior development** through the county squad programme
- Provide **safeguarding guidance**, codes of conduct and policies for clubs
- Represent Bedfordshire clubs and players with **England Squash**

## Our area

Bedfordshire squash covers Bedford, Luton, Dunstable, Biggleswade, Flitwick and Leighton Buzzard, and the leagues also include clubs from Milton Keynes.

## Get involved

The association is run entirely by volunteers. If you can help with coaching, refereeing, organising events, junior development or looking after this website, please [get in touch](/contact/).
